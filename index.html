ABOUT TEDx Emirates Hills

Theme: Beyond Borders – Celebrating Diversity and Multiculturalism
Nov 21, 2026 • 03:00 PM – 07:00 PM

Ideas have no borders. Perspectives know no boundaries. And the most meaningful change often begins when people, cultures, and ideas come together.

On November 21, 2026, TEDxEmiratesHills returns to Dubai for its next inspiring edition, embracing the theme “Beyond Borders: Celebrating Diversity and Multiculturalism.”

In a city that brings together people from every corner of the world, this edition celebrates the power of diversity, the beauty of different perspectives, and the connections that unite us beyond geography, culture, language, and identity.

Through thought-provoking talks, inspiring personal journeys, and powerful storytelling, TEDxEmiratesHills 2026 will bring together visionaries, innovators, changemakers, and passionate individuals who challenge conventional thinking and offer fresh perspectives on the world around us.

More than an exchange of ideas, the event is an opportunity to build bridges across cultures, encourage meaningful conversations, and explore how our differences can become a source of collective strength. It is a celebration of shared humanity, creativity, collaboration, and the possibilities that emerge when we look beyond boundaries.

About TEDx, x = independently organized event

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. At a TEDx event, TED Talks videos and live speakers combine to spark deep discussion and connection. These local, self-organized events are branded TEDx, where x = independently organized TED event. The TED Conference provides general guidance for the TEDx program, but individual TEDx events are self-organized.

About TED

TED is a nonprofit, nonpartisan organization dedicated to discovering, debating and spreading ideas that encourage conversation, deepen understanding and drive meaningful change. The organization is devoted to curiosity, reason, wonder and the pursuit of knowledge — without an agenda. TED welcomes people from every discipline and culture who seek a deeper understanding of the world and connection with others.
