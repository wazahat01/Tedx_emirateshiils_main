Theme : Beyond Borders – Celebrating Diversity and Multiculturalism

November 21, 2026 | SEE Institute, Dubai, UAE

Ideas have no borders. Perspectives know no boundaries. And the most meaningful change often begins when people, cultures and ideas come together.

On November 21, 2026, TEDxEmiratesHills returns to Dubai for its next inspiring edition, embracing the theme “Beyond Borders: Celebrating Diversity and Multiculturalism.”

In a city that brings together people from every corner of the world, this edition celebrates the power of diversity, the beauty of different perspectives and the connections that unite us beyond geography, culture, language and identity.

Through thought-provoking talks, inspiring personal journeys and powerful storytelling, TEDxEmiratesHills 2026 will bring together visionaries, innovators, changemakers and passionate individuals who challenge conventional thinking and offer fresh perspectives on the world around us.

More than an exchange of ideas, the event is an opportunity to build bridges across cultures, encourage meaningful conversations and explore how our differences can become a source of collective strength. It is a celebration of shared humanity, creativity, collaboration and the possibilities that emerge when we look beyond boundaries.

A Global Platform for Ideas

Inspired by TED’s mission, “Ideas Worth Spreading,” TEDxEmiratesHills is part of the global TEDx programme — a platform for independently organised events that bring the spirit of TED to local communities.

Following the successful editions in 2024 and 2025, TEDxEmiratesHills continues its journey of creating a space where remarkable ideas meet diverse perspectives, where stories spark conversations and where one idea can inspire meaningful change.

About TEDx, x = independently organized event

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. At a TEDx event, TED Talks videos and live speakers combine to spark deep discussion and connection. These local, self-organized events are branded TEDx, where x = independently organized TED event. The TED Conference provides general guidance for the TEDx program, but individual TEDx events are self-organized. (Subject to certain rules and regulations.)

About TED

TED is a nonprofit organization devoted to Ideas Worth Spreading, often in the form of short talks delivered by leading thinkers and doers. Many of these talks are given at TED Conferences, intimate TED Salons, and thousands of independently organized TEDx events around the world. Videos of these talks are made available, free, on TED.com and other platforms. Audio versions of TED Talks are published to TED Talks Daily, available on all podcast platforms.

TED's open and free initiatives for spreading ideas include TED.com, where new TED Talk videos are posted daily; TEDx, which licenses thousands of individuals and groups to host local, self-organized TED-style events around the world; the TED Fellows program, which selects innovators from around the globe to amplify the impact of their remarkable projects and activities; The Audacious Project, which surfaces and funds critical ideas that have the potential to impact millions of lives; TED Translators Program, which crowdsources the subtitling of TED Talks so that big ideas can spread across languages and borders; and the educational initiative TED-Ed. TED also offers TED@Work a program that reimagines TED Talks for workplace learning. TED also has a growing library of original podcasts, including The TED Interview with Chris Anderson, WorkLife with Adam Grant, Far Flung with Saleem Reshamwala and How to Be a Better Human.
